Entrepreneurship at ERABOTICS

Every invention gets pitched

We don't teach entrepreneurship as a separate subject — it's built into every stage of the Pathway. Every project starts from a problem worth solving, and ends with presenting it in front of others.

Where it shows up

Woven through every stage of the Pathway

💡

A problem, not just a project

Weekly builds — a traffic light, a recycling sorter, a smart farm — start from a real-world problem to solve.

🎤

Demo Day

Every 8-week course ends with kids presenting their own invention to parents — real pitching practice, from the first stage.

📜

Portfolio & certificates

Every level ends with a certificate, building toward the kind of project portfolio universities look for.

🏆

A competition path

Top students get a path into competitions — testing an idea in front of more than just family.

🌍

Global Pathway

Our oldest students (15–18) build the strong project portfolio used in international university admissions.

🤝

Teamwork

Many projects are built and presented in small teams — a habit every real venture depends on.

🚀

A dedicated Entrepreneurship curriculum is on our roadmap

Today, entrepreneurial thinking is built into how every course runs — not taught as a standalone subject yet. A dedicated track is something we're planning for the Pathway's future.

Have an idea worth building?

Tell us about the student and the kind of idea they're interested in — we'll suggest a starting point in the Pathway.
