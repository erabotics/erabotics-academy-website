03 How it works

Learning by building,
not by watching.

01 / 04
  1. 01 — Learn

    Two focused, hands-on sessions every week.

    Taught at your school by engineers — not lecture-style. Every session moves straight from idea to hands-on work.

  2. 02 — Build

    8 weeks. 8 finished projects.

    Students apply what they learn to something they build themselves — a finished project every single week.

  3. 03 — Use real tools

    Real hardware. A real fabrication lab.

    Students work with real components and ERABOTICS' own fabrication lab — not toy substitutes.

  4. 04 — Show & celebrate

    Every 8 weeks ends with Demo Day.

    Students present their own invention to their parents — explaining how it works and why it matters.
