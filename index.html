Our name

What ERA stands for

ERABOTICS isn't just a name — it's what we believe students need: to build, to understand AI, and to think like innovators.

  1. E

    Entrepreneurship

    Students learn to turn an idea into a plan, and a plan into something they can pitch and ship.

  2. R

    Robotics

    Mechanics, electronics, and control — students design and build robots that actually move.

  3. A

    Artificial Intelligence

    From how machine learning works to giving a robot the ability to see and decide.

The Academy

One clear journey, by age

From first build to university-ready portfolio — five stages, each with its own projects and pace.

  1. 01

    Ages 4–6

    Little Explorers

    Screen-free coding with command cards. Kids build simple robots and grow logical thinking through play.

    2 levels
  2. 02

    Ages 7–10

    Robot Builders

    Block coding, circuits, motors, and sensors — race cars, traffic lights, and line-followers.

    3 levels
  3. 03

    Ages 11+

    AI Innovators

    From Scratch to Python and C, then real AI: image and voice recognition.

    6 levels
  4. 04

    Ages 12+

    Future Engineers

    PCB circuit design, simulation with Proteus, and 3D modeling & printing.

    2 levels
  5. 05

    Ages 15–18

    Global Pathway

    Prepares students for international admissions tests like TMUA, ESAT, and SAT for CS, AI, and engineering.

    3 levels

Every stage runs 8 weeks, 2 sessions a week, ending in a finished project and a Demo Day for parents.

How it works

Learning by building, not by watching

  1. 01

    Learn

    Two focused sessions a week, taught hands-on at your school — not lecture-style.

  2. 02

    Build

    8 weeks, 8 finished projects. Students apply what they learn to something they build themselves.

  3. 03

    Use real tools

    Students work with ERABOTICS' own fabrication lab and real hardware, not toy substitutes.

  4. 04

    Show & celebrate

    Demo Day: kids present their own invention to parents at the end of every 8-week course.
