About ERABOTICS

A place to build brave young minds

ERABOTICS Academy is a robotics and STEM education center founded by engineers, dedicated to nurturing the next generation of engineers, innovators, and problem-solvers through hands-on, project-based learning.

Our name

What ERA means

E — Entrepreneurship

We believe building something is only half the skill — students also need to know how to shape an idea, validate it, and explain why it matters. Every project ends with presenting it, the way an inventor pitches an idea.

R — Robotics

Robotics is where abstract ideas become physical. Mechanics, electronics, and control systems give students a direct, tangible way to see their thinking work — or fail, and learn why.

A — Artificial Intelligence

AI is no longer optional literacy. We teach it as something to understand and build with, not just a tool to consume — from the fundamentals of machine learning to deploying a model on real hardware.

Our mission

Make hands-on technology education accessible to the next generation

ERABOTICS exists to give students a way to learn robotics and AI by actually doing them — building real projects with real tools, not just following along with slides.

The Academy is the center of what we do. Everything else — our fabrication lab, our store, our partnerships — exists to support that learning, not to compete with it.

Values

What guides how we teach

Learn by building

Every program is project-based. Students build something real, not just complete exercises.

Real tools, real skills

Students work with the same fabrication and prototyping equipment our team uses, not toy versions of it.

Entrepreneurial from day one

Students learn to pitch and defend their ideas, not just build them — a habit, not a single course.
