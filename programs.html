Programs & Courses

The ERABOTICS Pathway

One clear journey — from first build to university-ready portfolio. Every course runs 8 weeks, 2 sessions a week, ending in a finished project and a Demo Day for parents.

01 · Ages 4–6

Little Explorers

Screen-free coding with command cards. Kids build simple robots, learn how things move, and grow logical thinking through play.

LEGO / ZMROBO kit included — your child keeps it.

Screen-free · Simple logic2 levels
02 · Ages 7–10

Robot Builders

Block coding, circuits, motors, and sensors — 3 levels. Weekly projects like a Race Car, Smart Traffic Light, Night Lamp, Recycling Sorter, and a Line-Follower Robot.

Block coding · Sensors3 levels
03 · Ages 11+

AI Innovators

From Scratch to Python and C, then real AI: image and voice recognition — 6 levels. Projects like a face-recognition door, a voice-controlled car, and a smart farm.

Python & C · AI concepts6 levels
04 · Ages 12+

Future Engineers

PCB circuit design, simulation with Proteus, and 3D modeling & printing — 2 levels. Final project: their own circuit board inside their own 3D-printed case.

PCB design · 3D modeling2 levels
05 · Ages 15–18

Global Pathway

Prepares students for international admissions tests such as TMUA, ESAT, and SAT, used by top universities in the UK, USA, and Europe for CS, AI, and engineering.

Portfolio · TMUA · ESAT · SAT3 levels
Elective · Ages 14–18

Voice Over

Build confidence speaking and presenting — voice control, clarity, and performing in front of a microphone or a camera.

Communication skillsContact us
