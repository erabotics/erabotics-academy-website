Artificial Intelligence Track

Understand AI, then put it to work

From how machine learning actually works to giving a real robot the ability to see, recognize, and decide — AI taught as something you build, not just something you use.

What you'll learn

Inside the AI track

📊

AI & ML fundamentals

What machine learning is, how a model learns from data, and where it succeeds or fails.

👁️

Computer vision

Teaching a system to recognize objects, track motion, and interpret a camera feed in real time.

🐍

Programming for AI

Python and the core tools used to build, train, and test AI projects from scratch.

🦾

AI on real robots

Deploying a trained model onto a robot built in the Robotics track, so it can see and react.

📟

Edge & embedded AI

Making AI work within the memory and power limits of small boards and microcontrollers.

🧩

Applied final projects

Students choose a real problem — vision, prediction, or automation — and build a working solution.

Path

How the track progresses

01

Foundations

Programming basics and an intuitive introduction to how AI and machine learning work.

02

Computer vision

Build projects that detect, track, and classify what a camera sees.

03

Applied & embedded AI

Take a model beyond a laptop — optimize and deploy it to real, resource-limited hardware.

04

Capstone project

Combine everything into one applied AI project, often paired with a robot from the Robotics track.
