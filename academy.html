FAQ

Common questions

Does my child need prior experience?+
No. Our foundation-level programs in every track assume zero prior experience. More advanced programs list prerequisites on their own page.
Do you provide the equipment?+
Yes — programs are designed around the tools and kits students need. Specifics are listed on each program's page.
Can our school book a custom program?+
Yes. Visit Schools & Partners or contact us directly with your group size and goals.
Do robotics, AI, and entrepreneurship overlap?+
Yes, by design. Later-stage projects — like AI Innovators' face-recognition door or voice-controlled car — combine robotics and AI, and every project is presented at Demo Day the way an inventor would pitch an idea.
