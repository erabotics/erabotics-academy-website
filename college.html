College Track — Year 1 & Year 2

A programming-to-AI pathway for university students, built on the same hands-on, project-based approach as our school programs.

Year 1

Building the foundation

Year 1 · Course 1

Fundamentals of Programming

Core programming logic and problem-solving — the foundation for every course that follows.

Year 1 · Course 2

Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)

Classes, objects, and OOP principles for building structured, reusable software.

Year 1 · Course 3

Python

Python from the ground up — the language used across the rest of the track.

Year 2

From data to AI

Year 2 · Course 1

Data Science

Working with real datasets — cleaning, analysis, and drawing insight from data.

Year 2 · Course 2

Data Analyst

Practical data analysis and visualization for business and research decisions.

Year 2 · Course 3

Machine Learning (ML)

Core ML algorithms and how to train, evaluate, and apply models.

Year 2 · Course 4

Deep Learning (DL)

Neural networks and deep learning architectures, building on the ML foundation.

Year 2 · Course 5

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Bringing it together — applied AI concepts and hands-on projects.
